## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ultra-wide immersive web game background, no UI, no text. Create a completely new Eastern xianxia absurdist world called THE INVERTED SEA OF STARS, reached after an impossibly giant jade sword shatters an ice jade gate. Cinematic surreal concept art, extremely detailed crisp materials, luminous ice jade cyan with rich periwinkle, lavender, warm porcelain and a small contrasting molten peach horizon. A colossal translucent jade koi/kun whale swims sideways through a split ringed moon across the sky. Gravity is broken: enormous inverted crystalline mountains and ancient Chinese pavilions float upside down, waterfalls pour UPWARD into planetary rings, a winding ribbon of milky jade ocean curls like a Mobius strip into deep space. In the foreground lower edges, sharply fractured jade platforms suspended in the air, with a sweeping clear central flight corridor that leads toward the giant whale and the moon in the middle distance. The world should feel impossibly huge, playful, bizarre and breathtaking, like a beautiful cosmic catastrophe frozen mid transformation, not grim or scary. Distinct depth layers, precise sculptural surfaces, rounded translucent jade with visible internal inclusions, crisp high contrast details, no smeary haze. Bright otherworldly lighting, generous luminous areas, colorful not dark. Wide 16:9 landscape, 3840x2160 if possible. No sword in the image because the website renders the player's sword separately. No person, no text, no letters, no logo, no interface.
